# NyayaAI – Citizen & Legal Practitioner User Guide

## 1. Introduction

NyayaAI is an Indian Legal Intelligence platform created to help citizens understand their statutory rights and step-by-step procedures when facing legal disputes. It delivers clear, concise, visually structured legal intelligence without dense paragraphs of legalese.

---

## 2. Asking a Legal Question

### Best Practices:
1. **Be Specific About What Happened**: Mention amounts, dates, and modes of payment.
   - *Good*: "I paid ₹200 for my university exam fee via UPI on 10 November. The money debited, but college is refusing my hall ticket."
   - *Vague*: "College has an issue with my fee."
2. **Include Incident Dates**: The law changes over time. Specifying the date ensures the system applies the correct substantive law (for example, IPC 1860 for crimes before 1 July 2024 vs. BNS 2023 for crimes after 1 July 2024).
3. **Choose Your Language**: Select from English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, or Kannada from the top language bar.

---

## 3. Understanding Your Answer

Every response generated by NyayaAI is structured into actionable visual cards:

- **📌 Quick Summary**: 2–3 sentences giving an immediate, plain-language assessment of your situation.
- **⚖️ Applicable Law & Exact Sections**: Exact Acts, section numbers, and controlling Supreme Court / High Court judgments. Look for the visual badges:
  - `[STATUTE]`: Enacted laws passed by Parliament or State Assemblies.
  - `[JUDICIAL PRECEDENT]`: Binding Supreme Court rulings.
  - `[REGULATORY NORM]`: Binding administrative regulations (UGC, RBI).
  - `[PRACTICAL RECOMMENDATION]`: Real-world operational steps.
- **🗺️ Step-by-Step Action Plan**: Numbered escalation steps starting from internal complaint to statutory authority.
- **📋 Interactive Evidence Checklist**: Checkboxes of essential documents (payment UTRs, receipts, dispatch proofs).
- **⏳ Important Deadlines & Limitation Periods**: Exact statutory time limits to prevent your claim from becoming time-barred.
- **🚦 Do's and Don'ts**: Key precautions to protect your rights.
- **🏛️ Official Portals & Contact Helplines**: Verified government URLs (e.g., `consumerhelpline.gov.in`, `rtionline.gov.in`, `cybercrime.gov.in`, `samadhaan.ugc.ac.in`).
- **💼 When to Consult an Advocate**: Objective criteria for when you should engage a licensed legal practitioner.

## 4. Generating a Formal Complaint Notice

Click the **"📝 Generate Legal Notice / Complaint"** button under any answer to automatically draft a formal notice:

1. Select your dispute category:
   - *College Examination & Fee Dispute (UGC Regulations 2023)*
   - *Cheque Bounce Demand Notice (Section 138 NI Act)*
   - *Consumer Forum Complaint (Section 35 Consumer Protection Act)*
   - *Cybercrime & UPI Fraud Bank Notice*
   - *Rental Security Deposit Refund Notice (TNRRLTA Section 10(2))*
   - *Unpaid Salary Formal Demand (Payment of Wages Act)*
   - *RTI Application (Section 6(1) RTI Act)*
2. Fill in the relevant names, addresses, amounts, and dates.
3. Click **"Generate Complaint"**.
4. Review the generated notice, copy it to your clipboard, or print/save as PDF for dispatch by Registered Post with Acknowledgment Due (RPAD).
