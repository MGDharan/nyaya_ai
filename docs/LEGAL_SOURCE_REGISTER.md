# Legal source register — initial authoritative discovery list

| Source | Scope | Use | Verification note |
|---|---|---|---|
| https://www.indiacode.nic.in/ | Central, State and UT legislation | Primary consolidated legislation discovery | Verify Gazette publication and latest commencement/amendment |
| https://egazette.gov.in/ | Gazette of India | Authoritative publication of Central notifications and enactments | Capture Gazette date, part, section, number and PDF |
| https://legislative.gov.in/ | Legislative Department | Bills, Acts, legislative updates | Cross-check Gazette and official text |
| https://www.ugc.gov.in/ | Higher education | UGC regulations and notices | Check applicability and current amendments |
| https://www.sci.gov.in/ | Supreme Court | Judgments and court information | Capture case citation, date, bench and later treatment |
| https://ecourts.gov.in/ | Court services | Court and case information | Not a substitute for official judgment text |

Do not scrape websites contrary to their terms, robots rules, rate limits or copyright restrictions. Store source URL and retrieval timestamp for each item. This list is not a statement that all listed sources have been ingested.
